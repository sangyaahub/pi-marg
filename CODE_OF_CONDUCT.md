# Code of Conduct

PiMarg welcomes people of every background and experience level. Be respectful, assume good intent while checking facts, critique ideas rather than people, and keep discussions focused on improving the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberately disruptive conduct are not accepted in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to `contact@sangyaa.co`. Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the community. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is representing PiMarg or Sangyaa in public project activity.
